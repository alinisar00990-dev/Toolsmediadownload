Why does the PDF compressor not work on some files?
Our PDF compressor works by rendering each page of your PDF as an optimized image and rebuilding the document. However, if your PDF is password-protected, encrypted, or contains advanced DRM (Digital Rights Management) restrictions, the browser's security policies prevent our tool from accessing the file content. In such cases, you'll need to remove the password protection first using your PDF reader software, then try compressing again.
