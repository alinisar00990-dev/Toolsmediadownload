Help Desk

Questions & Answers

Have questions about how our free tools work? Find quick answers below.

Is ToolsMediaDownloader really free?
Yes, 100%! All our PDF file conversion systems, compression logic, merger engines, and video downloader components are free to use without restrictions, subscriptions, or hidden charges.
Are my uploaded PDF files secure?
Absolutely. Unlike other platforms, all file conversions (JPG to PDF, PDF to JPG, Merge, and Compress) are computed locally inside your browser context. Your document bytes never touch our servers, protecting your private files completely.
How can I download videos without watermarks?
Simply copy the video URL from TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook, click on our matching Downloader card, paste your clean link, and hit Download. The engine will retrieve the raw high-definition video stream directly.
Why does the PDF compressor fail on some files?
Our compressor parses document parameters down page-by-page. If your original PDF is password encrypted or contains advanced DRM protection constraints, system libraries block direct processing access.
Do I need to create an account to use the tools?
No, you never need to sign up, log in, or provide any personal information. All tools are instantly accessible from any browser on any device. Just open the page and start using.
What file formats are supported?
Our PDF tools support JPG, JPEG, PNG images for conversion, and standard PDF files for merging, compressing, and extracting. Video downloaders support direct URLs from TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook.
Can I use these tools on my phone?
Yes! ToolsMediaDownloader is fully responsive and optimized for all devices including smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers. All tools work seamlessly across all screen sizes.
Is there a file size limit?
Since all processing happens in your browser, the limit depends on your device's memory and browser capabilities. Most modern devices can handle files up to 50-100MB without issues. For very large files, we recommend using a desktop browser.
