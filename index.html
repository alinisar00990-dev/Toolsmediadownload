About Tools Media Downloader

Who we are and what we stand for

Tools Media Downloader is a small independent project that builds simple, free tools for people who work with images, PDFs, video and text. We started it because many everyday jobs, like shrinking a GIF or merging two PDFs, shouldn’t need an account or a download.

The site is supported by advertising, which keeps every tool free. We aim to keep pages fast, clear and honest about what each tool can and cannot do. Questions or ideas? Get in touch or read more about us.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about how the site works

Are these tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use, with no account, no signup and no usage limit. The site is supported by advertising.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

The image, GIF, PDF and text tools run in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Which file types are supported?

PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP and GIF images, MP4 and WebM video, PDF files and plain text.

Can I download any video with the downloaders?

Only download content that you own or have permission to use. Respect the copyright and the terms of each platform. We do not host any videos.

Does this site use cookies and ads?

Yes. We use Google Analytics to understand usage and Google AdSense to show ads. Read the Privacy Policy for details and your choices.

How do I report a problem or request a tool?

Use the Contact page. We read every message and use your feedback to decide what to fix or build next.
