Last updated: 2026

At ToolsMediaDownloader (toolsmediadownload.online), the privacy of our visitors is of extreme importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information that is received and collected by our website and how it is used.

We highly respect your privacy. All of our PDF tools (Convert, Merge, Compress) run entirely inside your web browser using JavaScript. Your files are NEVER uploaded to our servers. We do not store, view, or share your documents.

Like many other Web sites, ToolsMediaDownloader makes use of log files.
