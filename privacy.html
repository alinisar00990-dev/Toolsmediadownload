Privacy Policy

At ToolsMediaDownloader, accessible from toolsmediadownload.online, your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information processed when you use our website.

1. Local File Processing (PDF Tools)

All PDF and document tools (including JPG to PDF, PDF to JPG, and Merge PDF) process your files locally inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Your files are never uploaded to or stored on our servers.

2. Video Downloader Services

When using our media download tools (such as the TikTok Downloader), the video URL you submit is processed securely via remote APIs to generate download links. We do not host, archive, or keep logs of downloadable media files on our infrastructure.

3. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use standard technical cookies or basic browser local storage to maintain session states. We do not track personal identifying information across external websites.

4. Third-Party Services

We may link to third-party endpoints or APIs to provide functionality. We encourage users to consult the privacy policies of these third-party platforms for detailed information.
