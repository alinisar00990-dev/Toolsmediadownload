1. Overview

ToolsMediaDownload ("we," "us," or "our") operates a collection of free browser-based tools including media downloaders, file converters, and utility applications. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how your information is handled.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how it is used, and what choices you have regarding your data. By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Most of our tools process files entirely in your browser. Your files are not uploaded to our servers unless explicitly required for a specific tool's functionality.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

  • URLs you submit — When using our downloader tools, you paste a URL that is sent to third-party APIs to fetch the media link.
  • Contact details — If you reach out through our contact form, we receive the name, email, and message you provide.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data — Pages visited, time spent, referring pages, and browser type. This helps us understand how the site is used and improve it.
  • Device information — IP address, browser version, operating system, and screen resolution, collected in aggregate for security and analytics.
  • Files you process — For client-side tools (image converter, GIF compressor, etc.), files never leave your device. They are processed entirely within your browser.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our tools and process your requests
  • To understand usage patterns and improve our tools, content, and user experience
  • To monitor and analyze trends and track usage of our website
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or security concerns
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our website and store certain information. Cookies are small files that may be used to store information on your device.

Types of cookies we use:

  • Essential cookies — Required for the website to function correctly. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — Help us understand how visitors interact with the site so we can improve it.
  • Preference cookies — Remember your settings and choices (such as theme or language) for a better experience.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, some parts of our website may not function properly.

5. Third-Party Services

We rely on third-party services to operate certain features of our website. These services may collect information governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Media download APIs — Downloader tools send URLs to external API providers (such as RapidAPI, Cobalt, and TikWM) to retrieve media links. These providers may log the URLs you submit.
  • Analytics providers — We may use services like Google Analytics to understand site usage. These providers collect anonymous usage data in accordance with their privacy policies.
  • CDN and hosting providers — Content is delivered through content delivery networks and hosting services that may log technical data for security and performance.
  • Font and icon providers — Google Fonts and FontAwesome are loaded from external CDNs, which may receive your IP address.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

For our client-side tools, the strongest protection is that your files never leave your browser in the first place. What stays on your device cannot be intercepted by us.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access — You can request information about the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — You can request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion — You can request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal obligations.
  • Opt-out — You can disable cookies in your browser settings and opt out of analytics tracking.
  • Withdraw consent — You can withdraw any consent you previously provided at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take steps to delete such information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

If material changes are made, we may also provide a more prominent notice, such as on the homepage or via email (if we have your contact information).
