4. Local File Processing (PDF & Media Tools)

We highly respect your privacy. All of our media downloader tools (TikTok, Facebook, Instagram) process links through secure third-party APIs, and the downloaded videos go directly from the source server to your device. We do not store copies of any videos you download on our servers.

Like any other website, we use "cookies" to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.
