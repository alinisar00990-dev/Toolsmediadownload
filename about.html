About Tools Media Downloader

Free, fast online tools for creators, built by a small independent team.

Our mission

We believe useful tools should be free and easy to use. Tools Media Downloader gives creators, marketers and everyday users fast browser-based tools for images, GIFs, PDFs, QR codes, text and more, with no account and no install.

Most of our tools run in your browser, so your files stay on your device. The social media downloaders work differently: they send the link you paste to third-party services that locate the media file. Our Privacy Policy explains exactly what is and is not shared.

What we value

  • Privacy first: we keep data collection to a minimum and explain it clearly.
  • Speed: tools that open fast and give results right away.
  • Free to use: every tool is free. The site is supported by advertising.
  • Simplicity: clean pages that anyone can use.
  • Respect for copyright: download only content you own or have permission to use.

What we offer

  • Instagram, Facebook and TikTok downloaders for content you have the right to save
  • GIF Compressor and Video to GIF
  • Image Converter (PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP) and Image Resizer
  • PDF Tools: merge, split and convert images to PDF
  • QR Code Generator
  • Text Tools: count, transform and clean text

Who runs this site

Tools Media Downloader is a small independent project. Real people read every message sent through the contact page, and your feedback decides which tools we fix or build next.
